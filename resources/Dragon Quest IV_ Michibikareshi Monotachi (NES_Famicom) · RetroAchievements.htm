    Mastery #27, and the last of the NES DQ games done as well. Easily the best of the NES DQ games and one of the best RPGs on the NES. The AI will always be a problem for this version of it, as you can only take so much of Cristo trying to cast Beat on bosses repeatedly for you to just say "Fine, I'll heal as the hero, in the wagon you go", and that causes the non-Hero boss fight to be rather RNG reliant. The set overall is very well designed, the only missables being the two medals before the lighthouse, and a solid guide through the game. There isn't much grinding needed for mastery beyond what you would already do (and maybe a bit of Casino beyond the cheevo to get yourself some end game gear early). One thing the AI does is make grinding really easy, especially with a team of Mara/Ragnar/Alena, who consistently murder enemies (Especially if you get Mara the Metal Babble Shield early from the Casino so she doesn't take a ton of damage from attacks).

  • BigBomba88

    Mastery #35!

    Honestly, I was not that big of a fan when I first played Dragon Quest 4 (I played the DS version first.)
    But after my journey playing through each of the original games, something just clicked with me about it.

    It has a very unique structure, and it's expanded upon EVERY aspect of the games so far, probably pushing the limits on what they could have accomplished on the NES!

    I have heard people complain about the tactics AI, and I agree that SOMETIMES it will go against what you want, but for the most part if you are smart about it and change the tactics based on the battle at hand, you can make it more consistent.

    I actually think that here, the tactics system enhances the game entirely, it truly makes for a unique experience and forces you to strategize!

    I even used Kiryl in my main comp and he only tries to use instant kill spells on bosses with specific tactics, so change them around if he keeps casting it, other than that he was amazing the entire playthrough, consistently insta-killing basic encounters making it an easy grind.

    10/10, probably one of my favorite of the NES games now, and a strong contender for the rest of the franchise!
